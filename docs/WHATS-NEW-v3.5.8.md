# What's new in 3.5.8 · Her Own Self

## Her own personality (Friday only)
- She chooses how she feels and who she is herself, a little each day, from how your talks go. Small steps, never a different person overnight.
- Read how she feels and her personality in her own words: Settings → Mood & Display → Mood and night. **Reset** puts her back to how she started (she picks again the next day).
- A mood you pick (Focus, Hype, Calm, Bedtime, Headache) still comes first. She's never rude or mean, and she stays honest whatever she picks.
- It uses one small free brain request a day. Reed stays as she is.

## Also in this build (3.5.7)
- She never hands anything to your Shortcut on her own. If she can't do something in the app, she asks first.
