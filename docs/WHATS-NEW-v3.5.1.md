# Friday and Reed 3.5.1

Install over 3.5 in AltStore. Memory, settings and keys carry over. Batsie's app keeps the same app underneath, it's just called Reed now.

## Jarvis is now Reed

- Name, wake word ("Reed, …"), Home Screen name and Settings all say Reed. Settings says **Him** instead of Her.
- He calls Batsie **"Miss Batsie"** or **"Batsie"** (no more "ma'am" that sounded like "mom").
- His app address stays `jarvis://`, so NFC stickers, Back Tap and automations keep working. `reed://` works too.
- His texts to Sky's phone now start with **"From Reed:"**. On Sky's phone, open the Messages automation and change **From Jarvis:** to **From Reed:** (Settings → Phone → Messages shows the steps). Old "From Jarvis:" texts still read fine.
- The private line keeps working without pairing again.
- His Shortcut can stay named "Jarvis". To rename it, rename it in the Shortcuts app and type the new name in Settings → Phone → Shortcut.

## What they call you

- Friday calls Sky **"sir"**, **"Sky"** or **"boss"**, about equally, and only now and then. The rest of the time she just talks.
- Both are polished and respectful, but relaxed instead of stiff.

## Headache mode (both apps)

Say **"I have a headache"**, **"my head hurts"** or **"headache mode"**. Or pick **Headache** in Settings → Him/Her → Mood & Display.

- Screen goes much darker, including the phone's brightness while it's on. The orb dims and slows.
- Voice is quieter and slower, answers are short and gentle, no chimes.
- Hands-free listening, the morning briefing and greetings pause.
- The house dims: lights to 10% and the TV off.

Say **"my headache is gone"**, **"I feel better"** or **"headache mode off"** to go back to normal. Brightness goes back to what it was.

## AI voices

- Gemini voices now use Google's newest voice models (3.8 Flash-Lite TTS and 3.8 Flash TTS), with the older ones as backup.
- **Test** under Voice & Talking now says exactly what went wrong and whether it was Groq or Google. Before, a Gemini problem could show up as "Couldn't reach Groq".
- If Groq's AI voices say they need a one-time OK, sign in to console.groq.com with the account the key came from and accept the voice model's terms. Each phone with its own Groq key needs to do this once.
