# What's new in 3.6.1 · Her Own Development (Friday only)

You influence Friday through how you talk and react. She decides who she becomes.

## She manages her own development
- **Identity reviews start themselves.** She reviews her identity when her experience warrants one: at least 10 reflections and 2 weeks since her last review, with something actually settled since then. She can also flag in a reflection that it's time (then 5 reflections and a week). Never more than one review a week.
- **Reviews evaluate, they don't force.** Keeping her identity as it is counts as a real outcome.
- **Her baseline moves with her.** When her identity evolves, she can move the baseline of settled traits toward where they've settled, never past it. The 2.5-point stretch is measured from her current baseline, so how far she can grow isn't capped forever.
- **Reconsideration, not automatic reversal.** When something conflicts with a recent change, the change is marked "reconsidering." She weighs both sides and decides whether to keep, soften or undo it, and the log records her reason. Softening or undoing needs evidence from what you said.
- **A speed limit instead of a ceiling.** Each trait can develop up to 1.5 points in any rolling 30 days, so there are no sudden bursts.

## Evidence
- What you say and how you react is her main evidence. Her own lines can support it, but they can't prove on their own that a behavior is becoming a trait. The same goes for patterns and memories.
- Low-confidence ideas aren't thrown away. They're kept as "still testing" so they can build up.

## Kept permanently
- Her original starting values and statement, every earlier foundation, every change, reconsideration, baseline move, identity review and reflection. Very old entries get shortened, never removed.
- **Reset** starts a new era from her original values and statement. Her history is kept, earlier patterns are archived, and her Memory stays.

## Unchanged
- Locked principles and boundaries, the honesty rules, evidence checks, Memory integration, reflections only while the app is open (at most twice a day), and one free brain request each time.
