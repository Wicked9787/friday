# What's new in 3.6 · Her Own Identity (Friday only)

## A steady foundation
- Her identity statement, conversational character, principles and boundaries. This is her anchor. It only changes through a deliberate **Identity review** (Settings), where she looks back over her settled changes and decides whether her core still fits. Principles and boundaries never change.

## A personality that develops through experience
- 18 traits: humor, wit, sarcasm, warmth, confidence, curiosity, assertiveness, composure, playfulness, sociability, directness, empathy, independence, creativity, reflectiveness, skepticism, patience, spontaneity.
- They shift a little at a time (at most half a point per reflection, at most 3 traits at once), never more than 2.5 points from her foundation, and only when something actually said in your conversations shows a lasting pattern. Today's mood doesn't count as lasting.
- A new change starts out **provisional**. If a later reflection supports it, it becomes **settled**. If a later experience points the other way, it gets **undone**. After a week without being contradicted, it settles on its own.
- She never changes just to please you or to be easier to agree with.

## Reflection
- Runs inside the app while it's open (iPhone gives the app no background time): the first time each day after a few messages, and at most once more later if you talk a lot. One free brain request each time.
- She reviews what happened, what she learned, which patterns showed up, whether she stayed true to herself, and whether she was repetitive, rigid, too agreeable, dismissive or too formal. Then she decides whether anything should change and why.
- Every reflection is saved as a record. She only says she has reflected or changed when a record shows it.

## Memory and patterns
- Running jokes, preferences, interests and styles that work are saved as observations. They stay "still testing" until they come back on different days, then become part of her. Ones that never come back fade after 3 weeks.
- Facts and shared moments from a reflection go into her existing Memory (no second memory system, no duplicates).

## Where to see it
- Settings → Mood & Display → Her own personality: her identity statement, mood today, maturity, every trait with its foundation mark, what changed and why, patterns, and her last reflection. **Reset** puts her back to how she started (her Memory stays).
- Neural map → Self: her mood each day and the patterns you've built grow as bubbles.

## Honest about what she is
- Traits are behavioral tendencies and moods are conversational moods, not human feelings or consciousness.
