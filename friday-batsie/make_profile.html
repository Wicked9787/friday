friday icon

This builds a small settings file for your iPhone. After you install it, Friday sits on the Home Screen with her own icon and opens full screen.

Usually https://wicked9787.github.io/friday/?u=sky — change the username if the site moves.

On the iPhone: open the downloaded file, then go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the Friday profile, and tap Install. Apple will say it is unsigned. That is normal for a profile you made yourself.
