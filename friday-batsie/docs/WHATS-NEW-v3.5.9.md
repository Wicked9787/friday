# What's new in 3.5.9 · Self in Her Brain

## Her Self in the neural map (Friday only)
- A new **Self** part of her brain, joined to her core (and to Memory and Voice). Tap it to see how she feels today and her personality in her own words.
- Each day she picks how she feels, a small bubble grows off Self ("playful", "calm"…), so over time you can see the trail of her moods. Tap a bubble to see the day.
