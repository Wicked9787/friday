# Friday and Reed 3.5.3 · No "robot" needed, call her from anywhere

Install over 3.5.2 (in LiveContainer pick the option that replaces the app, not "Install as new"). Memory, settings and keys carry over.

## No need to say "robot"

Once you've paired with the robot ("pair with the robot", tap Allow), just ask for the job:

- "open YouTube and search lo-fi music"
- "play Drake on Spotify"
- "turn on Low Power Mode in Settings"
- "check my order on Amazon"

She checks whether the request needs tapping inside another app. If it does and the robot is on, she says "I'll have the robot do that" and does it. Jobs like "open YouTube and search …" stay one job now instead of being split in two.

Lights, alarms, timers, reminders, texts, calls, weather, questions and just opening an app still work the normal way. Saying "robot," first still works. The same safety rules apply: she asks before Send/Pay/Buy/Delete/Post-type taps and never types passwords or codes.

## Call her from anywhere

The friday:// link can't reach an app that lives inside LiveContainer, so use LiveContainer's launch link:

1. In LiveContainer, press and hold Friday → Add to Home Screen → Copy Launch URL.
2. In Shortcuts, tap +, search "Launch App", pick the LiveContainer one, paste the link. Name it "Wake Friday" (not just "Friday": that name belongs to her phone-jobs Shortcut).
3. Use it with Back Tap (Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap), the Action button, or say "Hey Siri, wake Friday". You can also add it to the Home Screen.

These steps are also in Settings → Phone → Shortcut → Wake with Back Tap.
