# What's new in 3.5.7 · Ask First

## She asks before using your Shortcut
- She never hands anything to your Shortcut on her own anymore. If she can't do something in the app, she says so and asks "Want me to hand it to your Shortcut?" Say or type **yes** to hand it over, or **no** to leave it.
- This covers everything: lights or TV she can't reach, cameras, Do Not Disturb, texts she can't send from the app, long timers on your iPhone, and bubbles you tap.
- She won't say she did something on the phone unless the app actually did it, and she never offers to control locks, garage doors or alarms.

## Fixed
- Some requests (like "basic quick sweep of all of them") were sent to the Shortcut automatically with nothing for it to do. That can't happen anymore.
- Private-line messages never go through the Shortcut. If the app can't open the text itself, you get the Open in Messages button.
