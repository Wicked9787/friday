# Friday and Reed 3.5.2 · Pictures & Robot

Install over 3.5.1. Memory, settings and keys carry over. Both apps get everything below.

## Pictures

- Ask with **pictures**, **photos**, **images** or **"show me"**: "show me pictures of wooden stairs", "best way to build stairs, with pictures", "what does a capybara look like".
- She answers like normal and opens a **Pictures** panel with real photos. Tap a photo to see it big. Tap the small credit line under it to open where it came from.
- After talking about something, "show me pictures" works on its own. She uses what you were just talking about.
- Free: Openverse (openly licensed photos) first, Wikimedia Commons as backup. No key, no account, no trial.

## The robot (iCTRL)

iCTRL is a separate app that can tap, type and read the screen in other apps. With it running, Friday and Reed can do jobs inside other apps.

**Set up once**

1. Open **iCTRL**. If it says Not mounted, tap **Start local mount…** and **Mount** (needs Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN). Then tap **Start Server**.
2. Say **"pair with the robot"** and tap **Allow** on the robot's notification.

**Use it**

- **"Robot,"** then the job: "robot, open YouTube and search lo-fi music", "robot, open Settings and turn on Low Power Mode".
- **"Stop the robot"** cancels what it's doing. **"Is the robot on?"** checks. **"Forget the robot"** unpairs.
- She comes back to her own screen when the job is done, unless it's something you'll want to keep looking at.

**Safety**

- Before tapping anything like **Send, Pay, Buy, Delete, Post or Confirm**, she stops and asks. Say **"yes"** or **"go ahead"** to continue, or **"no"** to stop.
- She never types passwords, passcodes, PINs, card numbers or verification codes. She tells you to do that part.
- Each job stops after 16 steps or about 2½ minutes.
- iPhone shows **"Automation Running"** while the robot is on. Holding **both volume buttons** stops the robot any time. Start it again from iCTRL.
- After the phone restarts, open iCTRL, **Mount** again, then **Start Server**.

**Good to know**

- The robot only listens on the phone itself (127.0.0.1). Nothing from outside the phone can use it unless you turn on Remote Access in iCTRL, so leave that off.
- Reed has the same robot commands. They just say the robot isn't on unless iCTRL is set up on Batsie's phone too.
