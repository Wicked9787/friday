# What's new in 4.0 · Central Intelligence

## F.R.I.D.A.Y.
- Her name is written **F.R.I.D.A.Y.** everywhere you see it: the top of the screen, chat, Settings, the neural map, and the label under her icon. She still says it "Friday". Her links (friday://), your Shortcut, and messages with Reed don't change.

## One brain that picks the right brain
- Every request is sorted into a task: conversation, quick sorting, thinking things through, code, writing, research, web search, photos, her reflections, or summaries.
- Every brain she can use (each Groq model plus your backup brains) is rated for what it's good at: thinking, speed, code, writing, structured answers, seeing photos, and web search.
- For each request she ranks them by those strengths, how each has been doing (failures, speed, how it handles that kind of task), and how much of its free daily allowance is left. Then she tries them in that order and automatically moves to the next one that can do the job.
- A rejected key rests every model that uses it. Background work (reflections, learning, summaries) never uses a paid brain or the last of a free allowance.
- Adding a key in Settings adds a brain to the router. Nothing else has to change.
- Every brain gets the same personality and memory, built on your phone, so she's the same whichever one answers. Her reflections can now use any capable brain, not only Groq.
- Settings → Brains shows who she'd ask for each job and how much each brain has been used today.

## Research she can back up
- She checks several free sources at once: web search (Groq), Google Search through Gemini (if you've added a free Gemini key), Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo, and Google News for news questions.
- Every site is rated: highly reliable (government, universities, Reuters, AP, BBC…), reliable (major outlets, Wikipedia…), unrated, or weak (Reddit, social media, forums).
- The app itself decides what's verified: two different sites back it (at least one reliable), or one highly reliable site does. The AI can lower a rating but never raise it. Points from one site are "single source", points from weak sites are "uncertain", and points the sources disagree on are "disputed", with both sides kept. A point that cites evidence that doesn't exist is dropped.
- She answers in her own voice, leading with what's verified and saying plainly what isn't. Sources show under her answer (✓ = verified). Tap one to open it. They stay with her answer in the chat.
- If she can't reach any source, she says so instead of guessing.

## Memory that knows what it is
- Every memory has a type: **facts**, **preferences**, **temporary** (plans, appointments, how today's going), which clears itself when it's over, and **shared moments**.
- Older memories were sorted automatically.
- Every brain gets the same memory, picked for what you're talking about right now.
- Statements about who she should be ("Friday should be meaner") are never filed as memories. Her personality only changes through her own evidence-checked reflections.
- The Memory panel is grouped by type.
